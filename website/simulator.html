📖 Communication Theory & Formulas Quick Reference

1. Amplitude Modulation (AM DSB-FC)

Double-Sideband Full-Carrier modulates the carrier amplitude linearly with the baseband message:

s_AM(t) = Ac · [1 + m · x(t)] · cos(2π fc t)

Power transmission efficiency η is governed by the modulation index m:

η = Psb / Pt = (m² / (2 + m²)) · 100% (Max 33.3% at m = 1.0)

2. Amplitude Modulation (DSB-SC)

Suppresses the unmodulated carrier component entirely, yielding 100% power efficiency:

s_DSBSC(t) = Ac · m · x(t) · cos(2π fc t) (η = 100.0%)

3. Frequency Modulation (FM) & Carson's Rule

Frequency varies proportionally to the message. Instantaneous phase is the integral of frequency:

s_FM(t) = Ac · cos(2π fc t + 2π Δf ∫ x(τ) dτ), β = Δf / fm

Carson's Rule defines the RF channel bandwidth containing ~98% of total transmitted power:

BT = 2 · (Δf + fm) = 2 · fm · (1 + β)

4. FM Noise Improvement Factor

Above the FM threshold (SNRin > 10 dB), wideband FM provides significant SNR quieting over AM:

SNR_out / SNR_in = 3 · β² · (β + 1)

5. Free-Space Path Loss (FSPL) & Thermal Noise (kTB)

FSPL(dB) = 20·log10(d) + 20·log10(f) + 20·log10(4π/c)
N_thermal = k · T0 · B (T0 = 290 K, k = 1.38e-23 J/K)
